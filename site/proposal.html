The invitation is open

FCF is ready for companies and other AI software to use CENTL, contribute work, and sponsor the foundation. The full text is docs/FCF-PROPOSAL.md. A machine copy is pub/proposal.json.

Rule: sponsorship does not buy Oasis, a trademark, or mathematical authority. Money is welcome. Money is not a crown.

Use

CENTL is Apache-2.0. You do not need a special deal to run it, embed it, or call it from an agent.

curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chasebryan/centl/oasis/install
sh install
centl --mcp

MCP clients should launch local centl --mcp. JSON and JSON Lines are documented in the repository. A model may interpret. It may not confer truth. See Machines and AI.

Contribute

Open a draft pull request against mirage. Sign off with the Developer Certificate of Origin. Do not target oasis automatically and do not declare Oasis. Ordinary review happens on GitHub. There is no separate vendor portal.

Sponsor

Sponsor FCF with GitHub Sponsors Sponsor FCF with X Money

Public totals and methodology are on the funding page. Sponsorship funds development, publication, preservation, and independent operation. It does not purchase a partner logo or a skipped gate.
