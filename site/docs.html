Contribute and support

Contributing to CENTL includes code, tests, mathematics, review, documentation, preservation, reproducible bug reports, and infrastructure.

CARAVAN participation is itself a contribution. When public enrollment is opened through qualified rollout channels, volunteer carriers can contribute bounded storage, bandwidth, and availability to approved FCF artifacts. Until then, carrier preparation and join-path testing are useful development contributions.
