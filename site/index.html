Exact Mathematical Matrix

Arbitrary-precision rational arithmetic, coordinate-free relativistic mechanics, and discrete consequence calculus.

1. Arbitrary-Precision Rational CAS

Exact BigRational core eliminates floating-point rounding errors. Features canonical polynomial division, Rational Root Theorem factorization, and symbolic calculus with mathematical certification.

Architecture Numerical Guarantees CAS Grammar

2. Stoichiometric Chemical Balancer

Nullspace linear algebra engine for reaction stoichiometry, molecular formula parsing, oxidation-reduction balancing, and reaction thermodynamics with exact rational molar ratios.

Stoichiometry Manual Chemistry Engine

3. Bryan Recursive Entanglement Calculus (BREC)

Canonical v1.0 specification of the discrete consequence calculus. Deterministic multi-agent state tracking, finite consequence bounds, and algebraic entanglement invariants.

BREC v1.0 Specification Technical Preprints

4. Erdős–Straus Program & Distributed Hunt

Investigation of 4/n unit-fraction representations across arithmetic progressions. Type-II geometry, congruence obstruction shadows, and distributed cluster verification.
