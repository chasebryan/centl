Core Research & Computational Modules

1. Exact Computer Algebra & Polynomial Factoring

Arbitrary-precision rational arithmetic engine (BigRational). Canonical polynomial division, Rational Root Theorem factorization, and symbolic calculus without floating-point truncation.

Formal investigation of 4/n unit-fraction representations across arithmetic progressions. Type-II geometry, congruence obstruction shadows, and distributed cluster verification.

Canonical specification v1.0 of the discrete consequence calculus. Deterministic multi-agent state tracking, finite consequence bounds, and algebraic entanglement invariants.
