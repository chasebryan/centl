Preservation, not promises

A link is not a mirror.

FCF is building an upstream-loss-resistant preservation layer for CENTL and the external sources needed to build and verify it. The Bazaar distinguishes material that is actually preserved from material that is only referenced upstream.

The rule: a dependency is not called mirrored until FCF has the bytes, an immutable identity, and an integrity record.
