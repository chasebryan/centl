Release: CentL26.12-GL (v26.12.0)

Date: 2026-08-23

Executive Summary

CentL26.12-GL ships the God's Letter hunt as an unsolved-only watchdog on the Erdős–Straus conjecture, with corrected CC.kernel linear identities and a fail-closed vault that refuses to file a solved 4/p identity as a God's Letter.

A God's Letter is a Mordell-hard prime — residue 1, 121, 169, 289, 361, or 529 (mod 840) — that escapes the linear sieves 4p+3, 3p+2, 8p+5 and still has no exact witness 4/p = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z after the full engine menu. Solved identities are not God's Letters.

This release does not claim a proof of Erdős–Straus. Origin census through 18.1 × 10^6: 1,159,667 primes, 100% verified, 0 God's Letters.

Key Highlights

  • God's Letter hunt filter; no auto-start; soft reset.
  • Corrected even / 4p+3 / 3p+2 / 8p+5 identities in BigInt. Live CC share 87.5%.
  • Live engine HUD percentages and witness SHA-256 certificates.
  • Unsolved Mordell primes are not admitted as letters.
