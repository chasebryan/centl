🐪 Prepare your camel

Public enrollment is still being provisioned. Once the signed public join release and authenticated coordinator are live, this is the whole normal path:

  1. Read the short host contract below.
  2. Accept it on behalf of a machine you control.
  3. Continue to the exact FCF-signed release and run its guided join-caravan launcher.

The launcher handles the checks, mission choice, resource limits, local setup, and final authenticated enrollment. It records the policy acceptance locally and shows how to leave. No repository checkout or special tooling is needed.

Short host contract

By joining, you choose to contribute bounded storage, bandwidth, and availability to authenticated FCF public-approved material. Your home files are not cargo. The carrier runs rootless, opens no public inbound port, accepts no arbitrary uploads, and publishes no hostname, username, IP address, or public node listing. You may leave using the supported withdrawal command.
