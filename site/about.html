Institutional Mission

The Free Computation Foundation (FCF) operates as an autonomous scientific research institute and technological advancement society. We believe computational tools must remain transparent, deterministic, mathematically honest, and completely sovereign from proprietary cloud lock-in.

Our flagship computational platform, CentL26, proves that modern scientific software can achieve world-class speed, exact rational arithmetic, stoichiometric chemistry, and coordinate-free mechanics without ever manufacturing mathematical certainty or relying on opaque remote servers.

Core Scientific Tenets

1. Mathematical Honesty

Scientific computation must explicitly distinguish verified exact quantities from topological approximations. Never manufacture confidence or truncate digits silently.

2. Zero-Cloud Autonomy

Researchers must own their tools completely. All CentL26 algorithms execute 100% locally on the user's hardware with zero network dependencies.

3. Cryptographic Archival

Published theorems, decomposition certificates, and software builds are content-addressed and mirrored globally across the CARAVAN mesh.

4. Open Science Licensing

All core algorithms and software are released under the permissive Apache-2.0 open source license to advance global technological capability.
