Free computation, defined

Free computation is computation that people can inspect, run, reproduce, and build on. At FCF, “free” describes the freedom to study and use the software—not a promise that every calculation is simple or that every result is automatically correct.

Why CENTL exists

CENTL is an exact-first mathematics, physics, and scientific computation system. It keeps rational results exact when possible, makes approximation explicit, and documents the boundary between verified, computed, and indeterminate results.
