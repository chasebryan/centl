Transparency methodology

The figures above are manually reported sponsorship contributions received by the Free Computation Foundation. They are intended as a public sponsorship transparency record, not a complete financial statement, tax filing, or accounting ledger.

Amounts are reported in USD and are updated when sponsorship receipts change. Supporter identities remain private unless a supporter has independently made that support public or has explicitly authorized disclosure.

A machine-readable copy of the current figures is available as funding.json.
