Scientific Research Sponsorship

The Free Computation Foundation accepts philanthropic funding and research grants to support open algorithmic development, computational cluster infrastructure for large-scale prime verification, and deterministic software preservation.

How Capital Is Deployed

1. High-Performance Prime Search Clusters

Dedicated compute hardware for running distributed verification of Erdős–Straus unit-fraction decompositions and Collatz 2-adic trajectories.

2. Mathematical Software Engineering

Full-time engineering on CentL26 exact CAS algorithms, native AppKit/WebKit UI optimization, and cross-platform binary toolchains.

3. CARAVAN Distributed Mirror Mesh

Decentralized storage nodes ensuring immutable preservation of scientific papers, software binaries, and mathematical certificates.

4. Open Science Fellowships

Grants supporting independent mathematicians, theoretical physicists, and compiler architects contributing to open deterministic research.

Institutional Inquiries

Organizations and philanthropic foundations wishing to sponsor specific research programs or establish dedicated cluster grants are invited to open an issue or contact our maintainers on the CENTL GitHub Repository.
