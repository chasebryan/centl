Research library

The front of the library is a subject catalog, not a wall of hundreds of individual records. Only subjects with an existing public research corpus are listed here. Papers, theorem notes, certificates, and source directories remain available through the links inside each subject.

Program

Erdős–Straus and unit-fraction decomposition

Exact decomposition certificates, Type-II geometry, Type A/B boundary structure, hard-prime filters, CBX/CBIS machinery, and the current theorem frontier.

  • Program overview and current status
  • Current research frontier
  • Hard-smooth Type-II obstruction

Research topic

Character methods

Quadratic-character routing, shields, residue support, saturation, obstruction completeness, and character-conditioned reductions inside the Erdős–Straus program and CENTL-CBX research.

  • Character-shield obstruction completeness
  • Classification of Jacobi-saturated Type A/B layers
  • CENTL-CBX research corpus

Research topic

Shadowing, ancestry, and covering structure

Direct-shadow relations, multiplicative ancestry, divisor-child structure, Class-C escape, complementary covers, and the limits of universal shadow completeness.

  • Divisor-child ancestry theorem
  • Direct-Shadow Completeness: candidatewise finite attack
  • Constructive counterexample to universal Direct-Shadow Completeness

Research topic

Exact filters, selectors, and residual geometry

Exact q-shift filters, D-selector constructions, binary rescue families, residual corridors, saturation barriers, and non-divisibility-comparable Type-II certificates.

  • Exact q=7 strong/Type-II filter
  • Exact 2p+1 factor filter
  • D-selector and residual research corpus

Directed signed histories, recursive closure, re-entry, collision semantics, history invariants, and higher-dimensional entanglement geometry.
