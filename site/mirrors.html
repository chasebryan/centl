Bazaar policy

The Bazaar lists places from which FCF material can be obtained. A stall supplies bytes. Authenticated metadata defines which bytes CENTL trusts. The contracts are in CARAVAN.md and SUPPLY-CHAIN.md.

Current public state: CENTL v0.15.0 Al-Nur is the current Oasis release. Dedicated public artifact origins are still being provisioned. This page will not claim those bytes are publicly supplied until they are reachable and pass their audit gates.
