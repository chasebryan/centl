Exact finite containment graph

The later signed-box layers are not weak. Their novelty is distributedly shadowed by several earlier layers.

Canonical repository note

Claim boundary: these are exact finite set relations on the stated 10M domain. They are theorem candidates, not universal shadow theorems.

Finite graph

The standalone relation set contains 534,037 exact (k,p) hits across 100 admissible shifts and all 20,513 Mordell-hard primes through 10,000,000.

Only 16 layers add new first-hit coverage in increasing-k order; the other 84 are fully contained in the union of earlier layers.

No simple containment

\#\{T_k\subseteq T_j:j<k\}=0.
\#\{T_k\subseteq T_a\cup T_b:a,b<k\}=0.

So the 10M shadow structure is not a stack of duplicate layers and does not collapse into complementary pairs. A two-layer relation seen on the 100K smoke corpus disappears at scale.

Strong pairwise overlap, but incomplete

The best single predecessor still leaves a substantial residue. For example, T_11 contains about 78.22% of T_55, but not all of it. Other strong finite overlaps include 119←23, 95←47 and 143←47.

Why this matters

The standalone census showed that every layer through 399 is productive by itself, including every layer above the first-hit record 107. The overlap graph explains the apparent paradox: these layers can be intrinsically strong and still contribute zero new first hits because their hit sets are distributed across the earlier union.

That moves the theorem hunt away from “weak later layers” and toward exact containment structure, repeated complementary covers, defect/spectrum explanations and mathematically safe hybrid scheduling.
