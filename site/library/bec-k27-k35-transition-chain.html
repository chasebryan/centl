Status: exact chained transition theorem inside the candidate decomposition framework

Date: 2026-08-16

Verifier: verify_bec_k27_k35_transition_chain.py

Depends on: BEC-K27-K31-TRANSITION-THEOREM.md, K35-TWO-BRANCH-SURVIVOR-THEOREM.md, exact post-k23 ancestry

Claim boundary: this theorem selects the live k27, k31, and k35 transitions on h169 from exact factor-support predicates. It is not factorization-free, does not prove that every branch reaches k35, does not establish a universal depth bound, and does not prove Erdős–Straus.

1. Starting state

Assume

p = 169 + 840t

and that the branch is live after k23.

Use

C27 = 7E,    E = 7 + 30t
C31 = 10D,   D = 5 + 21t
C35 = 3F,    F = 17 + 70t.

The first two exact predicates are already defined:

G27(E)   k27 survivor grammar
Q31(D)   every prime factor of D is a nonzero QR mod31.

They give

R   iff not G27(E)
LR  iff G27(E) and not Q31(D)
LL  prefix iff G27(E) and Q31(D).

We now resolve the next rung exactly.

2. Exact k35 predicate M35(F)

K35-TWO-BRANCH-SURVIVOR-THEOREM.md proves that k35 misses exactly on the union of two factor-support branches.

Define

\boxed{M_{35}(F)=J_{35}(F)\lor S_7(F)}.

J35(F)

Every rational prime factor q of F satisfies

\left(\frac q5\right)\left(\frac q7\right)=+1.

Equivalently, every prime factor residue belongs to the exact index-two subgroup

H35 = {1,3,4,9,11,12,13,16,17,27,29,33} mod35.

S7(F)

There is exactly one prime factor q occurring to exponent1 with

q = 3 mod7,

and every other rational prime factor of F is

1 mod7.

Then the exact landed theorem is

\boxed{ k35\text{ misses}\iff M_{35}(F). }

Therefore

\boxed{ k35\text{ hits}\iff\neg M_{35}(F). }

3. Exact three-rung BEC selector

For every h169 branch live after k23:

\boxed{ R \iff \neg G_{27}(E). }
\boxed{ LR \iff G_{27}(E)\land\neg Q_{31}(D). }
\boxed{ LLR \iff G_{27}(E)\land Q_{31}(D)\land\neg M_{35}(F). }

and the deeper live residual has prefix

\boxed{ LLL \iff G_{27}(E)\land Q_{31}(D)\land M_{35}(F). }

The exact selector can therefore be written

if not G27(E):
    R at k27
elif not Q31(D):
    LR at k31
elif not M35(F):
    LLR at k35
else:
    LLL and enter the k39+ residual.

This is now a genuine arithmetic transition chain indexed by BEC words.

4. Exact residual after LLL

A branch with live prefix LLL has survived k23, k27, k31, and k35.

It carries

B_support   QR23
E_state     one of the seven exact k27 survivor modes
D_support   QR31
F_state     J35 OR S7

and the coupled companion identities

7E - 6B  = 1
10D - 7E = 1
5D - 3B  = 1
F - 2B   = 1
3F - 7E  = 2
3F - 10D = 1.

The dynamic cofactors B,E,D,F are pairwise coprime.

This is the exact mathematical payload behind the three leftward BEC transitions.

5. Finite ancestry regression

On the exact 148-survivor q23 ancestry specimen, the independent verifier reconstructs the three predicates from factorization and obtains

R      64
LR     64
LLR     7
LLL    13

without using the later first-hit category to choose the predicted class.

Thus the three exact selectors account for

64 + 64 + 7 = 135

of the 148 finite survivors by a terminal certificate through k35.

Only 13 enter the deeper LLL residual.

The finite count is a regression result. The selector equivalences themselves come from the range-free exact fixed-shift theorems.

6. Structural meaning

The live BEC history is no longer merely retrospective telemetry.

For the first three post-k23 rungs it is generated by exact predicates:

G27(E)     controls L/R at k27
Q31(D)     controls L/R at k31 after k27 survival
M35(F)     controls L/R at k35 after k31 survival.

Each L therefore has a precise surviving arithmetic state attached to it.

The BEC grammar is functioning as intended:

exact state -> exact theorem -> directional transition -> smaller residual.

7. Next target

The correct next object is the 13-branch LLL residual, not the full prime population.

The landed ancestry audit shows its terminal support is

k39   4
k43   5
k47   3
k55   1.

The next theorem search should therefore intersect the exact residual state

QR23(B)
G27(E)
QR31(D)
M35(F)

with the existing k39, k43, and k47 exact modules before introducing any broader search.

The goal is another selector theorem of the form

LLL + exact residual predicate -> LLLR at k39

or a still smaller surviving state.

That is how the cross becomes a machine: every leftward obstruction must carry forward a sharper exact payload until a rightward certificate is forced.

Erdős–Straus remains open.
