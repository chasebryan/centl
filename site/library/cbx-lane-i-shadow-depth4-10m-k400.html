Result

There are 84 layers whose complete finite hit set is contained in the union of all smaller shifts. Exhaustive search finds no exact earlier subcover of size four or less for any of them.

\boxed{\text{depth 1}=0,\quad\text{depth 2}=0,\quad\text{depth 3}=0,\quad\text{depth 4}=0.}

Thus every globally union-shadowed layer has finite shadow-depth at least five, if it has a finite exact subcover at all.

Why 107 is not a shadow target

Contrast with Spectrum C

The global graph needs depth at least five, while conditioning on Spectrum C reveals the exact finite depth-three relation

H_{363,C}\subseteq H_{23,C}\cup H_{39,C}\cup H_{59,C}.

This is strong evidence that useful symbolic absorption may need spectrum or residue information rather than a bare global containment.
