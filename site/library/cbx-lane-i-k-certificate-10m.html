Exact finite certificate

The smallest admissible signed-box Lane-I ceiling sufficient for every Mordell-hard prime through 10,000,000 is 107.

Canonical repository note

Claim boundary: this is a finite Lane-I certificate. It is not a universal K law, not the minimum complete CBX grade, and not automatically the minimum shared cbis scalar K.

Certificate

K_I^{min}(10^7)=107.

The finite target universe contains 20,513 Mordell-hard primes. Every one has a signed-box first hit by 107.

Why 107 is minimal

The unique record target is

p=8,803,369,\qquad k_I^*(p)=107.

That prime has no admissible Lane-I hit through 103. Since 103 is the previous admissible ceiling, 103 is insufficient while 107 is sufficient.

The record-prime gauntlet

After k=59, every other hard prime through 10M is already covered. The record prime survives 63, 67, 71, 75, 79, 83, 87, 91, 95, 99 and 103, then hits at 107.

Why this is not “the cbis K”

cbis exposes one scalar K but feeds it to both signed-box Lane I and López Lane L. CBX separates the finite grade as Γ=(F,K_I,E_N,A_L). This page certifies only the K_I coordinate.

Reproduce

./centl es cbx certify-i \
  --hi 10000000 \
  --i-max 400 \
  --segment 1000000 \
  --json

The certificate command cross-checks independent p-major and shift-major exact first-hit maps and hashes the canonical finite sets.
