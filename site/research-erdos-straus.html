1. Program Scope & Formulation

The Erdős–Straus conjecture asserts that for every integer $n \ge 2$, the rational quantity $4/n$ decomposes into the sum of three positive unit fractions:

4/n = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z

The FCF Research Program builds upon López Type A/B solution forms, establishing minimal first-hit witness depths $C_{AB}(p)$, exact layer cardinality, congruence shadow trees, and finite Direct-Shadow Completeness (DSC) certificates.

2. Public Distributed Prime Hunt

CentL26 embeds the exact native solver and public search engine for decomposing primes:

# Solve single prime decomposition:
centl26 "es solve 1009"

# Scan distributed prime window:
centl26 "es hunt 10000000"
