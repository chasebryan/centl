Executive Overview: The CentL26 Flagship Platform

CentL26 represents the Free Computation Foundation's flagship scientific computing architecture for 2026. Designed from the ground up in high-performance Rust, CentL26 replaces legacy heuristic computer algebra systems with a deterministic, mathematically provable rational core. Exact quantities remain exact throughout all transformations; when approximations are explicitly requested, they carry certified topological interval enclosures.

Core Architectural Modules

1. Arbitrary-Precision Rational Core (BigRational)

Eliminates IEEE 754 floating-point truncation. Every integer, rational quotient, and radical polynomial coefficient is represented as an irreducible pair of arbitrary-precision integers.

Numerical Contract

2. Canonical Polynomial Algebra & Root Isolation

Synthetic division, rational root theorem factorization, exact radical roots for degrees 1 through 4, and Sturm sequence isolation for higher-degree polynomials.

Algebra Syntax

3. Stoichiometric Chemical Matrix Balancing

Exact nullspace decomposition of atomic composition matrices. Supports nested polyatomic groups, molecular mass summation, and acid-base equilibrium calculations.

Chemistry Reference

4. Coordinate-Free Mechanics & Relativistic Kinematics

Strictly typed physical quantities with dimensional verification. 1D/2D elastic collisions, relativistic Lorentz invariants, and astronomical conversions.

The Exact Numerical Contract

  • Literal Invariance: Decimal literals (e.g. 0.1 + 0.2) parse as exact fractions (1/10 + 1/5 = 3/10) without binary conversion error.
  • Closure Under Rational Operations: Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and non-zero division over rational numbers produce exact rational outputs.
  • Certified Enclosures: Transcendental functions ($\pi, e, \sin, \cos, \ln$) compute to arbitrary precision with guaranteed interval bounds.
  • Visible Failures: Dimension mismatches or mathematically undefined evaluations fail explicitly rather than manufacturing false precision.
