The standard native archive is GNU/Linux x86_64 and installs:

- `centl`
- `centl-physics`
- `centl-sci`

MIRAGE, CARAVAN, FCF Camps, and CENTL Marsa stay outside the installed
product. Their source may be present. Presence is not Oasis assurance.
