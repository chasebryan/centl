## Executive Summary

**CentL26.12-GL** ships the **God's Letter** hunt as an unsolved-only watchdog on the Erdős–Straus conjecture, with corrected CC.kernel linear identities, live engine-share HUD, and a fail-closed vault that refuses to file a solved `4/p` identity as a God's Letter.

A **God's Letter** is a Mordell-hard prime — residue \(p \equiv 1, 121, 169, 289, 361,\) or \(529 \pmod{840}\) — that escapes the linear sieves \(4p+3\), \(3p+2\), \(8p+5\) and still has **no** exact witness \(4/p = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z\) after the full engine menu (CC + two-target corridor + bb). Solved identities are letters, remnants, or escapes. They are not God's Letters.

This release does **not** claim a proof of Erdős–Straus. An origin census through \(18.1 \times 10^6\) verified \(1{,}159{,}667\) primes at \(100\%\) exact identity and found **0** God's Letters.

## Key Highlights in CentL26.12-GL

### 1. God's Letter (Ω) filter
- Hunt filter **God's Letter Hunt** runs the full coordinated engine menu on every prime in the window.
- Persistence writes `gods-letter/GL-<p>.json` only when the candidate is Mordell-hard **and unsolved** after that menu.
- Solved letters, including the origin generator \(p = 2521\), are rejected as God's Letters.
- Observatory does not auto-start. The operator sets filter, engine, start, and window, then presses Start. Soft reset clears session HUD/ledger/pin without deleting vault files.

### 2. Corrected CC.kernel identities
Exact rational identities, overflow-safe in `BigInt`, verified before clearance:

| Sieve | Identity |
| :--- | :--- |
| even \(n = 2k\) | \(x = k+1,\ y = k(k+1),\ z = k\) |
| \(n \equiv 3 \pmod{4}\) | \(x = (n+1)/4,\ y = z = n(n+1)/2\) |
| \(n \equiv 2 \pmod{3}\) | \(x = (n+1)/3,\ y = n,\ z = n(n+1)/3\) |
| \(n \equiv 5 \pmod{8}\) | \(x = (n+3)/4,\ y = n(n+3)/8,\ z = n(n+3)/4\) |

The previous \(8p+5\) formula used \(x = (n+3)/8\) and failed \(4xyz = n(yz+xz+xy)\). Regression: \(p = 9341077\) is theorem clearance, not a CBIS escape.

Dirichlet density of the three odd sieves is \(7/8\). Live observatory CC share holds at **87.5%**.

### 3. Live engine HUD and certificates
- Engine cards report live shares of primes scanned (no hardcoded 95.2% / 4.2% / 0.6%).
- Pin SHA-256 is the witness certificate (`ES-WITNESS-v1`), not the empty-string hash.
- Unsolved Mordell primes are **not** admitted as letters (`MissingWitness`).
