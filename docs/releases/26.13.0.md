## Executive Summary

**CentL26.13-AG** turns the observatory into an **agnostic** proof ⊕ disproof machine. Every prime is filed on one of two ledgers. The hunt does not vote the conjecture true. Approximates never certify.

A **class proof** is an infinite residue identity (even, \(4p+3\), \(3p+2\), \(8p+5\)) covering **7/8** of the primes. An **instance proof** is one verified \(4/p = 1/x+1/y+1/z\). A **certified counterexample** is a complete finite region with no solution — one such ticket disproves Erdős–Straus. A **God's Letter** is an incomplete residual (menu miss), not a disproof.

Game status is **OPEN** until a covering of the six Mordell classes \(\{1,121,169,289,361,529\}\pmod{840}\) or one certified empty region.

- Weighing: one counterexample outweighs every instance pile; instance piles do not prove ES.

### 3. Approximates forbidden as certificates
