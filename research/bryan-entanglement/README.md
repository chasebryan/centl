# Bryan Entanglement Research

This directory is the canonical home of the **Bryan Recursive Entanglement Calculus (BREC)** and the **Bryan Entanglement Cross** that motivated it.

**Status:** active research program  
**Canonical frozen specification:** BREC v1.0  
**Freeze date:** 2026-08-16  
**Originator:** Chase Bryan  

## What BREC is

BREC is an abstract calculus for recursively directed constructive and obstructive consequences. Its primitive alphabet is signed, but its states are not merely signed. A state carries the complete finite history of signs that produced it.

The Bryan Entanglement Cross is the finite visual seed. BREC is the recursively closed mathematical object behind that seed.

## Research rule

BREC v1.0 is frozen. Future work may extend, specialize, or prove results about the calculus, but must not silently change the v1.0 definitions. Any incompatible change requires a new major version. Compatible additions must be explicitly versioned.

## Scope

The word *entanglement* in BREC names an abstract dependency and propagation structure. BREC does not, by itself, assert a model of quantum entanglement or any other physical phenomenon. A physical, mathematical, computational, or causal application must separately define what its states, signs, operators, weights, and observations mean.
