# Erdős-Straus — Honest Wall Statement

**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Status:** the conjecture is OPEN

---

## Statement

For every integer `n ≥ 2` there exist positive integers `x, y, z` with

\[
\frac{4}{n} = \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{z}.
\]

Step 1 is the present diamond edge (C1 → Class C → DSC-P).  
Step 2 is independent and open.  
Step 3 is largely classical but must be connected carefully.
