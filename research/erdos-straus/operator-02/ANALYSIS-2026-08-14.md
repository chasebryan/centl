## 3. Structural observations (Operator-02)

### 3.1 Locality of the obstruction

The guided coordinate-repair diagnostic (≤ 9 changes from a unary-safe basepoint, with ~37.9 % already solved at 0 changes) indicates that the globally large CRT systems are, in practice, low-dimensional once the right coordinates are identified. This is consistent with the peeling theorems and suggests that a classification of residual kernels may be feasible.

### 3.2 Fiber geometry as the decisive object

The move from coarse load λ_p to fiber load Λ_p is the most important theoretical refinement currently on the table. Any further progress on universal DSC-P is likely to require a deeper understanding of the possible fiber configurations that survive after all peelable coordinates have been removed.

### 3.3 Character shield as a global filter

The Jacobi −1 property is a uniform arithmetic constraint independent of the particular candidate. It can be used both as an elimination tool and as a structural constraint on admissible residual kernels. Combining it systematically with the fiber-kernel analysis appears high-leverage.

### 3.4 Ancestry families remain under-exploited for theorem extraction

The q = 5 family (k = 5j − 1) is the strongest observed uniform full-shadow family. A necessary-and-sufficient arithmetic condition for full / partial / absent shadowing under modulus ancestry would convert a large observed pattern into a theorem and simultaneously clarify the structure of the shadow graph.
