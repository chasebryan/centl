## 3. Operator-02 questions

The natural theorem target stated by the primary program is a necessary-and-sufficient condition on j for

\[
T_{5j-1}^{\mathrm{admissible}} \bmod (4j-1) \subseteq T_j.
\]

Operator-02 records the following concrete sub-questions that can be attacked from the arithmetic already present:

1. **Divisor interaction.**  
   Because T_k is generated by the divisors of k, and k = 5j − 1, the divisors of k are related to the divisors of j by the linear relation. Can the inclusion of reduced residues be rewritten purely in terms of the divisor lattice of j?

2. **Trap cardinality comparison.**  
   The exact trap-cardinality formula is known. Does a comparison of |T_k| and |T_j| under the relation k = 5j − 1 yield any necessary condition for full shadowing?

3. **Hard-class compatibility.**  
   The admissible candidates must also satisfy the hard-class congruence conditions modulo 840. Does the interaction of the 5-fold modulus relation with the fixed modulus 840 produce periodic restrictions on j that explain the observed mixture of full, partial and absent shadows?

4. **Character side.**  
   Since every trap lies on the Jacobi −1 side, full shadowing on an ancestry edge requires that every admissible t at layer k already lands on the Jacobi −1 side of m_j. Is this automatic under the relation m_k = 5 m_j, or does it impose an independent condition?
